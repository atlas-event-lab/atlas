# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Atlas to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for learning distributed systems,
regardless of experience level or background.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and patient, especially with people learning.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the learning community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, and publishing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer via
the contact on the organization profile. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
